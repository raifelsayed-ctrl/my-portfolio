05 / CUSTOMER SUPPORT CHATBOT

RayTech Support Chat

A bilingual customer support chatbot demo for an electronics store in Lebanon. It provides predefined answers about products, prices, available colors, delivery, payment methods and support contact details.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptNode.jsArabic / English

Project Overview

The interface supports Arabic (right-to-left) and English, conversation switching and locally saved chat history. Product questions can use follow-up context, and a server endpoint is available for optional AI integration when configured.

Key Features

Bilingual Interface

Switch between Arabic and English with matching text direction.

Product Questions

Answers for demo iPhone 17 and Samsung A57 pricing and available colors.

Lebanon Delivery

Demo delivery policy: a flat $5 across Lebanon.

Payment Options

Cash on Delivery and Whish Money.

Chat History

Multiple conversations saved locally in the browser.

Support Handoff

Unknown questions can direct customers to raytech@gmail.com when no AI response is available.
