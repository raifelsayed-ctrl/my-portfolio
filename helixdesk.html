01 / THE PLATFORM

Internal IT Service Desk.

HELIXDESK is an internal IT support management system designed to organize employee support requests in one central workspace. Employees can submit issues and follow their tickets, while support teams can manage and respond to requests through a dedicated dashboard.

02 / KEY CAPABILITIES

01 /

Ticket Submission

Employees can create support requests and describe technical issues.

02 /

Ticket Tracking

Monitor requests through clear ticket statuses and activity views.

03 /

Support Workspace

A central place for support staff to view and handle incoming tickets.

04 /

Role-Based Experience

Dedicated workflows for administrators, support staff, and employees.

05 /

Dashboard Overview

Quickly see ticket totals, open requests, work in progress, and overdue requests.

06 /

Request Priorities

Distinguish high-priority issues and keep support work organized.
