Project Overview

RAYTECH is an electronics e-commerce platform built to organize product browsing, purchasing workflows and administrative operations. It brings customer-facing shopping features together with tools for managing products and stock.

Product Catalog

Browse electronics products and view product details.

Inventory Tracking

Track stock availability through the system.

Admin Dashboard

Manage store-related data from a central interface.
